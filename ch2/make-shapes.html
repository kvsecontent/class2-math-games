Project work: make solid shapes with your own hands! Tap 🔊 to hear each step.

✂️ An object with four faces

➡️
  1. Take a cardboard sheet. Draw 4 triangles like this.
  2. Cut along the outside lines.
  3. Fold along the dotted lines.
  4. Bring the points together and tape them.
  5. Count the faces of your shape!

🟠 An object with one face

  1. Take some clay or dough.
  2. Roll it between your palms.
  3. Make it round like a ball.
